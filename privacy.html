Fleet ERP Privacy Policy

Fleet ERP is an internal tool used only by Yangin Cars Rental. It connects to the company's own Gmail inbox with read-only access to read rental, toll and reimbursement emails and record them in the company's private database.

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Data is used only to run the company's fleet operations. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
